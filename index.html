BEFORE YOU TAKE ACTION

Safety comes first.

AI guidance can help you understand a problem, but it cannot replace an on-site professional inspection.

Electrical issues

Stay away from exposed wires, sparks, burning smells, or wet electrical equipment. Do not open electrical appliances or outlets. Contact a qualified electrician.

Gas and fire risks

If you suspect a gas leak, avoid flames and electrical switches. Move away from the area and contact emergency or qualified gas services from a safe location.

Water and plumbing

Keep water away from electrical equipment. If safe and accessible, use the main water shutoff. Ask a plumber to inspect persistent or hidden leaks.

Structural damage

Do not enter areas with sagging ceilings, unstable walls, major cracks, or falling materials. Keep others away and seek a qualified inspection.

Remember: FixIt AI provides image-based estimates. A photo may not reveal hidden faults. Stop if a task feels unsafe and consult a qualified professional.
